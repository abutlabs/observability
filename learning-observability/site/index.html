JAM networks · any client mix

See inside a JAM network.

A practical course on the abutlabs observability stack: Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and Alloy, fed by each node's own metrics, its JIP-3 telemetry or its JIP-2 RPC. You learn what every piece does, how to read the dashboards, how a soak test judges a network, and how to find the cause when one fails.

What you will be able to do

Run the stack

Start it, check it, and know what each of its eight services does and which port it answers on.

Get any client in

Through its /metrics, its JIP-3 telemetry or its JIP-2 RPC, labelled so one dashboard shows every client.

Read the dashboards

Know the question each one answers, and write your own PromQL and LogQL when a panel is not enough.

Diagnose a failure

Follow a real failing soak from its symptom to its cause, then prove the fix with the numbers.
